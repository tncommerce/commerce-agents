# DUFYND image-rights evidence — priority live shortlist

Checked on 2026-09-26 for the five-product staging-to-live shortlist.

## Decision rule

An official manufacturer page is valid product-identity evidence, but it is **not** automatically an approved commerce-image source.

DUFYND may only promote a product with a product image after one of these paths is established:

1. an affiliate/merchant feed whose terms explicitly permit publisher use of the supplied product image,
2. a licensed media/press asset with commercial web-use permission,
3. explicit written permission from the relevant rights holder, or
4. a DUFYND-owned original product photograph or other asset for which DUFYND holds the required rights.

A public product page alone does not satisfy the image-rights gate.

## Operational conclusion

The five products remain strong live candidates because their exact variants and current direct purchase destinations are already documented in the parent work queue. However, **none of the five may use a copied public manufacturer-site image based on the evidence above**.

The fastest compliant route is therefore:

1. continue waiting for / checking approved affiliate or merchant product-feed access,
2. require the feed or partner terms to establish publisher image-use rights,
3. if no feed becomes available, request explicit image-use permission or create DUFYND-owned product photography,
4. visually verify the exact concentration, size, bottle and packaging,
5. only then transition the image approval state and rerun the promotion dry-run.

Finished social shorts for Libre and Mon Guerlain remain content assets only; they do not automatically satisfy the commerce-image gate.
