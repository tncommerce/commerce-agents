## Rights acceptance required

A candidate may enter the DUFYND 3D review queue only after the brand or rights holder confirms all of the following for the exact product variant:

- commercial use is allowed
- public distribution/display on DUFYND is allowed
- interactive web display is allowed
- the exact approved 3D asset is identified or supplied
- permitted formats are clear (prefer GLB/glTF; USDZ/FBX/OBJ acceptable only as source material if conversion is permitted)
- resizing/mesh optimization/format conversion permissions are clear
- texture/material optimization permissions are clear
- territory is clear
- duration is clear
- attribution requirements are clear
- revocation/replacement instructions are clear

Final geometry and visual fidelity approval remains human-only in DUFYND.
