I am contacting you on behalf of DUFYND, an independent fragrance discovery,

We would like to request written permission to use an official, unaltered
product packshot for the following exact product on DUFYND:

- Brand / product: [BRAND + PRODUCT]
- Concentration / size: [CONCENTRATION + SIZE]
- GTIN: [GTIN]

The image would be used to identify the product on DUFYND-owned fragrance
detail and comparison pages in a commercial publisher / affiliate context.
We would reproduce the supplied packshot accurately, without materially
altering the bottle, label, packaging or brand artwork, and without implying
that DUFYND is sponsored or endorsed by the brand.

Could you please confirm:

1. whether DUFYND may publicly display the supplied official packshot for this
   purpose;
2. which exact asset or media-library file we should use;
3. whether commercial / affiliate publisher use is permitted;
4. the permitted media, territory and duration;
5. whether attribution, a credit line or a link is required;
6. whether cropping, background removal or resizing is permitted;
7. whom we should contact if the permission or asset is later withdrawn or
   replaced.

If a press/media portal, retailer asset feed or separate licensing contact is
the correct route, please forward us or point us to that resource.

Thank you.

DUFYND / TNCommerce

## Acceptance checklist

Before converting any reply into a DUFYND rights-cleared candidate, record:

- exact product identity and GTIN;
- exact asset URL/file or media-library identifier;
- rights holder / authorizing party;
- date permission was granted;
- commercial public web use explicitly allowed;
- affiliate/comparison context allowed or not restricted;
- territory;
- duration / expiry;
- modification rules;
- attribution / credit requirements;
- removal / revocation conditions;
- original message or licence evidence location.

If any of these materially affect use and remain unclear, keep the product at
`licensed_source_required`.

## Operational rule

A written permission only clears the rights/source gate. It does not approve
the visual asset automatically.

After rights evidence is recorded:

1. register the exact asset as a review-only rights-cleared candidate;
2. verify bottle, concentration and size visually;
3. request human/user visual approval;
4. only then move the product image to an approved state.

No outbound request may be sent from this packet without explicit user
approval.
