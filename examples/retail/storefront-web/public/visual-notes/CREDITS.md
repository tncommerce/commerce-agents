## Historical botanical plates

The compact scent-world preview uses exact, unchanged 500-pixel Wikimedia thumbnails of three historical plates. CSS selects botanical details and mildly desaturates the presentation; the vanilla pod is rotated 60 degrees to show its whole form in the narrow horizontal preview. These are identified as botanical illustrations, not photographs, perfume formulas or product assets.

- **Vanilla**, Plate 114 (1890), *Köhler’s Medizinal-Pflanzen*: Brandt, Wilhelm; Gürke, M.; Köhler, F. E.; Pabst, G.; Schellenberg, G.; Vogtherr, Max.; digitisation by Biodiversity Heritage Library. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:K%C3%B6hler%27s_Medizinal-Pflanzen_in_naturgetreuen_Abbildungen_mit_kurz_erl%C3%A4uterndem_Texte_(Plate_114)_(8232785888).jpg). Source explicitly records CC BY 2.0 and PD-scan-old-expired. [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).
- **Rose**, Plate 124 (1890), same work, authors, digitiser and licence. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:K%C3%B6hler%27s_Medizinal-Pflanzen_in_naturgetreuen_Abbildungen_mit_kurz_erl%C3%A4uterndem_Texte_(Plate_124)_(8232791024).jpg).
- **Citrus limonum** (1887), Hermann Adolf Köhler (1834–1879). [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Citrus_limonum_(K%C3%B6hler).jpg), explicitly public domain (author life plus 100 years); [Public Domain Mark](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/).
