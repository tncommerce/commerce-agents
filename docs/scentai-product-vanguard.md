as an ultra-fresh fragrance.

### Sweetness — 4/10

- pineapple
- apple
- blackcurrant

However, the fragrance does not contain a dominant gourmand structure.

Sweetness remains moderate-low.

### Woodiness — 7/10

Strong supporting notes:
- cedarwood
- cashmere

- leather

### Spiciness — 6/10

cardamom, while Parfumo also lists those notes.

Spice is therefore clearly meaningful, but SCENTAI keeps the score below the
Absolu Aventus benchmark because of the current source inconsistency.
