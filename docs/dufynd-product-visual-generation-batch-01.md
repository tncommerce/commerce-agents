# DUFYND product visual generation · Batch 01

Prepared only. No paid generation is authorised by this plan.

The batch follows the new rule: generate a **verified product layer first**, then a separate DUFYND editorial world. Do not ask an image model to invent both product geometry and campaign styling in one pass.

## 1. Creed Absolu Aventus 100 ml

### Product-layer goal

Create a clean, premium isolated depiction for the DUFYND catalog and product hero.

Hard constraints:

- exact Absolu Aventus bottle family and current branding;
- intended catalog variant: 100 ml;
- no visible 75 ml / 2.5 fl oz marking;
- if the authoritative reference does not expose a trustworthy 100 ml volume marking, keep the lower bottle area volume-neutral rather than inventing text;
- black bottle, cap, CREED embossing and Absolu Aventus plaque must remain geometrically stable;
- no fruit, rocks, smoke or campaign scenery in the product layer;
- full cap and full bottle base visible with 12–15% safe area.

Editorial world after approval: **noir/mineral** — graphite stone, controlled grapefruit peel, dry woods, cool directional light. Product layer remains separate.

## Acceptance checklist for every product layer

Before a file enters `product_cutout_url`:

- silhouette side-by-side pass;
- cap/neck/shoulder pass;
- front label/plaque/logo pass;
- bottle colour/material pass;
- visible concentration/volume text pass;
- no invented secondary text;
- safe-area pass at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px;
- provenance recorded;
- product is still recognisable at catalog-card size.

A beautiful image that fails one of these product-truth checks remains editorial artwork.
