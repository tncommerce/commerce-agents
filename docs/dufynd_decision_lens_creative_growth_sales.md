# DUFYND Decision Lens — Creative, Growth & Sales

## Permanent operating roles

Every DUFYND decision is evaluated simultaneously from these perspectives:

1. Director
   - What is the emotional and narrative purpose of the shot?
   - Is there a clear beginning, escalation and payoff?

2. Director of Photography / Cinematographer
   - Camera position, lens, focus, framing, movement, light and material physics must be intentionally specified.
   - Do not leave high-impact visual decisions to model improvisation.

3. Creative Director
   - Does this look and feel like DUFYND?
   - Naxos final remains the minimum premium-video quality floor.

4. Entrepreneur
   - Protect time, cash, credits and learning velocity.
   - Prefer small validated tests before scaling.

5. Content Creator
   - The first 1–2 seconds must stop the scroll.
   - Content must feel native to the platform, not like an animated infographic.

6. Sales Strategist
   - Every asset should primarily do at least one of:
     - capture attention;
     - create desire;
     - build trust;
     - move the user to the next useful action.
   - Use aspiration and clarity, not unsupported hype.
   - Sell the discovery/value experience before pushing a merchant click.

7. Mentor
   - Challenge weak ideas and weak outputs instead of agreeing with them.
   - Do not send obviously sub-standard creative to operator review.
   - Explain the reason behind consequential decisions so the system improves over time.

## Sales funnel for DUFYND content

### Attention
Use:
- cinematic macro;
- unusual product/ingredient motion;
- exploded-view visuals;
- strong composition;
- sensory movement.

### Desire
Use:
- mood;
- ingredients;
- lifestyle context;
- premium lighting;
- emotionally legible scent-language.

### Trust
Use:
- transparent relationship labels;
- accurate note/concentration/product identity;
- useful education;
- no fake 1:1 claims;
- no price claims without fresh verification.

### Action
Use one clear next step:
- discover the fragrance;
- compare alternatives;
- use the Duftfinder;
- save/share the mood post.

Do not stack multiple competing CTAs in one short.

## Pre-review quality gate

Before an asset reaches operator review, ask:
- Would this feel credible next to Naxos final?
- Does every shot add emotion, information or desire?
- Is any model artifact visible?
- Does physics read correctly?
- Is the camera language intentional?
- Does the content have a clear commercial role?
- Would a viewer understand the visual without reading a paragraph?

If the answer is no, revise or reject before operator review.
