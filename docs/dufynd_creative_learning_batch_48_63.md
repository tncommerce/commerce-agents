## Example 59
Best prompt recovery in this batch.

Recovered partial prompt (normalized from readable on-screen text):
- `using Seedance 2.5`
- `shot: a small clear glass perfume bottle, 35ml, with a soft irregular hand-blown body that ripples like melting ice, thick uneven walls and visible internal distortion, filled with colourless liquid, topped with a chunky organic off-white matte stopper shaped like a rounded ice cube sitting slightly askew.`
- `Fine black serif lettering "The Lost" and "35ml 1.18 fl.oz" near the neck, and "I.M SOLE" in black on the lower body.`
- `Real glass with condensation, water beads and faint fingerprints.`
- `Keep the bottle identical in every appearance.`
- `@talent is the visual truth for the model in every shot:`
- visible talent description continues with a pale-skinned woman, flushed cheekbones, wet/slick dark hair, no visible jewellery, and a translucent-panel gown.

Learnings:
1. product locks can use tactile real-material language.
2. `@talent is the visual truth` applies the same continuity principle as `@sheet`.
3. realistic imperfections (condensation, droplets, faint fingerprints) can increase premium realism.
4. product and talent should each have their own identity anchor.
