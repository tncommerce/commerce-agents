session, even when its existing internal URL contained sid. The new isolated
regression failed before the correction: qa-new-transport-123456789 replaced
qa-original-funnel-123456789.

document referrer, after preferring the current memory/storage identity. It never
restores that ID as an API/advisor session. External, absent and deceptive-origin
referrers do not adopt it. Existing bounded retries and advisor ownership remain.

auxiliary-click time: their mount effect can precede session initialization.

unknown acquisition and no preceding fragrance_detail_view in its session.
