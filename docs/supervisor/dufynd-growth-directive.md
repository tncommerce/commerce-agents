# DUFYND Growth directive — owner decision, 2026-10-03

P0 remains the protected live read-only Control Room V1. Finish and verify it
before expanding scope. Jarvis's objective is measurable business impact with
low recurring cost, safe automation and less owner operation. Do not build
technology without a measurable purpose.

## Delivery order

| Phase | Outcome | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G0 Measurement | Connect content/campaign/platform/format/product/landing/merchant offer/clickref and timestamps to visits, product interest, clickouts and available conversion/revenue | Missing views, conversions or revenue remain unknown; distinguish evidence, latency and uncertainty |

Measure the full funnel: content → reach → DUFYND visit → product interest →
merchant clickout → affiliate conversion → revenue. Prefer qualified visits,
clickout and revenue value over views alone. Track asset/video costs, qualified
visitor cost and revenue by content, product, format and source. Reuse successful
assets, stop weak formats and strengthen proven formats using adequate evidence.

Optional owned audience (newsletter/alerts) and useful SEO can follow measured
opportunities. No paid infrastructure by default, thin content, mass spam or
unsolicited messages. Keep Growth, Content, Experiments and Monetization as
future additive dashboard projections rather than dummy sections now.

## Task decisions

Each future task should state its objective, hypothesis, measurable impact on
reach, visitor quality, conversion, revenue or measurement; cost; automation and
reuse potential; and owner time saved. Prefer robust inexpensive alternatives.
Jarvis must be able to learn from the result. Implement the next safe step after
V1 without another prompt when already authorized.

Stop for new spend, owner/security/auth decisions, credential requirements,
publishing, external messaging, irreversible actions or major business changes.
Do not modify `main`, PR #1 or PR #598. Existing owner-auth approval is limited to
the protected Control Room, not publishing, new provider access or general
content autonomy. This directive changes the roadmap, not the active queue.
