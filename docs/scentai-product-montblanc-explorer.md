## Score Reasoning

### Freshness — 9/10

Fresh and citrus are the two dominant community directions.

Strong supporting signals:
- bergamot
- aromatic sage

Explorer has a clearly fresh opening.

The woody and leathery structure prevents a maximum freshness score.

### Sweetness — 3/10

Sweetness is not a dominant community accord.

There is no strong gourmand structure.

The fruity impression adds some perceived softness and sweetness, but the
fragrance remains primarily fresh, woody and aromatic.

### Woodiness — 7/10

Woody is one of the major community accords.

Strong official support:
- cedarwood
- vetiver
- patchouli
- leather

Woodiness is an important part of Explorer's drydown and identity.

### Spiciness — 4/10

Pink pepper provides direct spice support.

Clary sage adds aromatic character.

However, spicy is not one of the dominant community directions.

The fragrance is therefore mildly to moderately spicy.
