## Score Reasoning

### Freshness — 8/10

Fresh and citrus are major community characteristics.

Strong official support:
- Calabrian bergamot
- grapefruit
- pineapple accord

Absolu Aventus remains clearly fresh, but its warmer and darker structure
reduces the freshness score compared with classic Aventus.

### Sweetness — 4/10

Pineapple provides fruity sweetness.

However, the fragrance is not dominated by gourmand or strongly sweet
accords.

Warm resinous elements add richness without making sweetness a defining
characteristic.

### Woodiness — 7/10

Woody character is an important part of the fragrance.

Strong supporting evidence:
- Haitian vetiver
- smoky woody structure
- labdanum-supported drydown

Woodiness is clearly more prominent than in a typical fresh-fruity fragrance.

### Spiciness — 8/10

Spice is a defining characteristic of Absolu Aventus.

Strong official support:
- cardamom
- ginger
- cinnamon

The community profile also identifies spicy character as one of the major
directions.
