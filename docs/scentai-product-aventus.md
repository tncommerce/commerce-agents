## Score Reasoning

### Freshness — 10/10

Fresh is the dominant community direction.

Strong supporting signals:
- citrus
- bergamot
- bright pineapple opening

Creed itself describes Aventus as fresh, citrus and fruity.

Freshness is therefore one of the defining characteristics.

### Sweetness — 3/10

Aventus contains fruity sweetness through the pineapple character and some
softness in the base.

However, sweet and gourmand accords do not dominate the community profile.

The fragrance is therefore classified as mildly sweet rather than sweet-driven.

### Woodiness — 7/10

Woody is a major community accord.

Strong official support:
- smoky birch
- dry woods

The woody structure is clearly important to the Aventus DNA.

### Spiciness — 2/10

Spiciness is not a defining community characteristic.

The current official Creed presentation also does not position spice as a
central part of the fragrance.
